What Founding Membership actually is
What it is

A standing seat, not a coupon.

Founding Membership is a permanent cohort marker layered on top of Quorum Elite — the tier that unlocks Mirror, Quorum's Judgment Operating System. It isn't a limited-time promo code that quietly disappears from your invoice later. Once you're in, your founding pricing and founding status stay attached to your account for as long as you remain subscribed.

In practice, that means two things: you get Elite's full feature set — Bias Fingerprint, Confidence Calibration, Contradiction Detector, and the rest of Mirror — at founding pricing, and you're recognized as part of the small group Quorum was actually built around in its early months.

Why now

Judgment infrastructure compounds. It needs real decisions early — not eventually.

Mirror gets sharper the longer it runs and the earlier real, high-stakes decisions start flowing through it. Founding Members are the first real record behind the product's own judgment — not a beta test, a starting point. Being early isn't a marketing angle here; it's structurally different from joining later, because the Judgment OS is literally built from accumulated decision history.

Who it's for

Not everyone. Deliberately.

A good fit

Founders, CXOs, and family office principals weighing decisions where being wrong is expensive — a stake sale, a succession call, an exit. People who already think carefully and want a system that keeps pace with that, and want to help shape the tool while it's still forming.

Probably not yet

If you're looking for a quick answer to a low-stakes, everyday choice, or a shortcut for avoiding the work of deciding, Founding Membership — and Quorum generally — isn't built for that. The free tier is still the right place to start and see for yourself.

Why limited

A small number, on purpose.

Mirror's earliest signal comes from real decisions, not synthetic ones. Keeping the founding cohort small means each person's feedback and pattern history actually shapes what gets built next, instead of being averaged into noise across thousands of accounts. It's a deliberate design constraint, not a scarcity tactic borrowed from a checkout page.

Once the cohort fills, it's closed — permanently. Even if a founding member later cancels, their seat isn't reissued to someone else.

Why early matters

The earlier you start, the further ahead your Mirror already is.

Because Mirror compounds off session history, someone who starts today ends up with a longer, richer decision record than someone who joins the identical product six months from now — same features, different starting line. Founding pricing is fixed for as long as you stay subscribed, so the earliest members get both a head start on their own Judgment OS and terms that won't come back around once the cohort is full.
