01
Identity

Quit the job — or keep building on the side?

First entry in the record · no revenue yet

Before there was a company to make resource decisions about, there was only this: commit fully, or don't.

Every advisor leaned the same way — wait. What read on the surface as a timing question was, underneath, an identity one: the pull toward the startup was emotional, the reasons to stay were structural.

"You're treating the startup's potential as a substitute for the stability you've already built."

02

This session opened with a direct line from the last one: "I decided to continue building the company full-time."

Resource allocation

Bootstrap further — or raise a seed round now?

$4.5k → $9k MRR · an angel offers an intro to seed funds

Revenue is real, and so is the fear of a market window closing.

Wait, again — not because the capital wasn't real, but because taking it now would hand roadmap control to someone else before the fit was proven.

"You're trading the fear of missing a crowded window for the clarity of proving what you've already built."

03

Opened the same way the last one closed: "I chose to continue bootstrapping."

Resource allocation

Hire a GTM leader — or keep selling personally?

$18k MRR · founder still closes every deal

It sounded like a hiring question. The record read it differently.

Wait, a third time — but this time the record named the real question directly: what does it cost you to be the one who always carries the weight of selling?

"You're outsourcing the weight of the decision before you've fully carried it yourself."

04

$30k MRR — nearly double the session before. Two enterprise customers now on the table.

Resource allocation · Challenging my thinking

Stay with one ICP — or expand into enterprise?

Decision record #4 · added to Mirror

The biggest number yet. The same question underneath.

For the first time, the record said the pattern out loud, unprompted: "the structural mechanism is identical to the earlier bootstrap-or-raise choice."

"You favor protecting proven patterns over scaling them, even when the alternative promises greater reach." — added to Mirror after this session.
