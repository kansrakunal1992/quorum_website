QUORUM WEBSITE v18 — PHONE OVERFLOW FIX (drop v2). Overlay on the v18 website zip.

REVISED  journey.html           #hero gets overflow-x: clip
REVISED  decision-library.html  #hero gets overflow-x: clip

Cause: the decorative gold glow (.hero-aura, 760-900px wide, positioned off-centre) stuck out past the right edge of the
screen on phones, making the page 260-470px wider than the viewport. body had overflow-x: hidden but html did not, and
mobile browsers can still pan or shrink the layout to fit. Clipping the hero sideways keeps the glow identical on screen
and stops it widening the page. "clip" (not "hidden") leaves vertical overflow alone, so the glow's upward bleed is kept.

Checked in Chromium at 320, 375, 414, 768 and 1280px wide on journey, decision-library and index: scrollable overflow is 0
at every width (was 263 and 468 at 375 on the two pages). index.html never had it.
