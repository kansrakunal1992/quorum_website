Quorum Website: v17 alignment edits (24 Nov 2026)
==================================================
ONE file changed: index.html   (26 lines added, 2 removed; nothing else in the site is touched)

Drop it into the website Railway project root, replacing the existing index.html, and redeploy. No new dependencies, no
environment variables, no SQL. server.js serves index.html as a static file and as the fallback page, so no code change is needed.

WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY
---------------------
1. Pricing, Free tier. Was: "A first look at Mirror after your third decision".
   Now: "A first look at your Mirror once your decisions support one: what Quorum has learned, what to watch, what to try next".
   Why: the app no longer makes people wait for a third decision. The Mirror opens with a free overview built only from what a
   person's decisions support, labelled early when the evidence is thin, and says so plainly when nothing is solid yet. "After
   your third decision" was out of date, and it understated what Free now includes.

2. Three-step line under the "Most AI / Quorum" comparison. Added a last sentence:
   "Then Quorum checks back on how it went."
   Why: the product's loop does not end at the decision. It records a check-in date and asks how it turned out. The page described
   the front half only, and the check-back is the reason to return.

3. FAQ, two new questions (and the same two in the FAQPage schema, so search results match the page):
   - "What happens after I decide?"  Commit, choose a check-in date, Quorum asks how it turned out and keeps the answer; early
     observations are labelled early; it does not claim to know you from one or two decisions.
   - "Do I need an account to start?"  No. Start without one. Linking an email keeps the record across devices and lets Quorum bring a
     decision back on the date chosen.
   Both answers describe what the app does today. See the dependency below.

WHAT I DELIBERATELY DID NOT CHANGE
----------------------------------
- The hero headline and sub-line ("See what you're actually deciding."), the title, the meta description, the audience copy, pricing
  amounts and tiers, the waitlist form, and every legal page. These are positioning and commercial decisions, not accuracy fixes, and
  the question of who the site and the app should be aimed at is open (see the separate point of view). Changing the headline now would
  mean changing it again.
- The site already names its audience in the FAQ ("Founders, CXOs, and family office principals ... It isn't built for everyday,
  low-stakes choices") and in "Quorum is built for decisions like these". That is the strongest audience copy in the whole product;
  the app front door is broader than it.

DEPENDENCY TO CHECK BEFORE PUBLISHING
-------------------------------------
"Do I need an account to start?" answers No. That is true for the first decisions today. If NEXT_PUBLIC_AUTH_GATE_MODE is still
"d3" in the app's Railway variables, a person is stopped at their third decision until they sign in. Remove that variable (unset
behaves as off) and redeploy the app with a fresh build, or soften this answer to "You can start without one."
